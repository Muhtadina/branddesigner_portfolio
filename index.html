About

I am Muhtadina Serniabat Tasin, a B.Sc. Student in North South University, majoring in Computer Science & Engineering. I am also newly recruited Cofounder of Defensive Women in Dhaka Division, the Cyber-security & Cyber Awareness Organization. My work blends technical structure with visual storytelling to build confident, scalable brand identities.

I am a Quick-Learner, Passionate & a Believer, eager to challenge thyself to the next level of improvement in Corporate Field as well as Computer Science, especially for Leadership. Willing to take on added responsibilities, seek and maintain an officer position that offers professional challenges utilizing interpersonal skills, excellent time management and problem-solving skills.

Known to be a workaholic person, prefer disciplined & organized work schedule, currently have several business ideas and meanwhile looking for a competitive partner. There are more project ideas involving web development and a longtermly research on mental health and ML. So far, working single handed on my projects but thriving for both success & excellence.

By the way, I am also leisure creative and script writer, passionate about writing thriller & fantasy stories and even poems. My imaginary world, with a hint of reality, is what I'd like to present through my writings. It's more of a metaphorical writing, and I do it as my inspiration to move forward in life.
